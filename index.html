My Projects

Smart Solar Cleaning System (SCC)

The Smart Solar Cleaning System is a project designed to help improve solar panel efficiency by reducing dust accumulation. Dust can affect the performance of solar panels, making regular cleaning important.

Our system uses a dust sensor to detect when cleaning is needed and automatically activates a cleaning mechanism. The goal is to make maintenance easier and reduce the need for manual cleaning.

Project Goal

To explore an automated solution for keeping solar panels clean and supporting efficient solar energy generation through smart technology.

Plant Care App — Prototype

This project is a mobile application prototype designed to make gardening easier, especially for people living in Saudi Arabia. It helps users discover plants suitable for their region and learn how to take care of them.

The app includes personalized gardening preferences, a plant catalog, planting recommendations, and information about watering, sunlight, soil, temperature, and harvesting.

It also explores features such as planting calendars, gardening reminders, weather alerts, and educational content about Saudi agriculture.
